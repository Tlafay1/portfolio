# My internship at ENE

## Overview

An internship focused on internal infrastructure penetration testing. My mission: assess the attack surface of the information system from the position of an attacker with access to the internal network.

## What I did

### Mapping and enumeration

Discovering the network and exposed services with `nmap`, then targeted enumeration of the identified services to build an actionable map of the infrastructure.

### Active Directory

Hunting for vulnerabilities and misconfigurations in the Active Directory environment: password policy, accounts and privileges, and possible attack paths toward privileged accounts.

### Reporting

Writing an audit report ranking the vulnerabilities by severity, with remediation recommendations that technical teams can act on.
